Knuckle-House

A stylized myth-tech boxing roguelike blending Greek mythology, "Punch-Out!!" style cinematic fights, and RPS (rock paper scissors) mechanics[cite: 1]. Currently in the prototype phase, built in Unity 6.3 for PC[cite: 1].

The World & Loop

Project Highlights

Designed to satisfy the high market demand for technical, physics-based melee combat (similar to titles like Sifu)[cite: 1].

  • The Roguelike Loop: Players navigate procedural node maps, challenge bosses on each floor, manage persistent health, and unlock meta-progression upgrades[cite: 1].
  • Myth-Tech Setting: The visual identity blends ancient pantheon aesthetics with futuristic sci-fi and heavy cybernetic enhancements[cite: 1].
  • The Narrative: Set in a dystopian future where the Gods of Olympus compete for the throne every year in a "fair" Rock-Paper-Scissors tournament[cite: 1]. Representatives fight to make their God the most popular, securing the throne for the next 5 years[cite: 1].

Combat & AI Systems

Frame-Precise Brawling

The moment-to-moment combat is built on traditional 3D fighting game fundamentals[cite: 1].

  • RPS to 3D Brawling: Encounters initiate with a strategic Rock-Paper-Scissors entry phase that dictates the opening momentum before seamlessly transitioning into real-time combat[cite: 1].
  • Group AI Director: To handle the chaos of group fights, a central AI director coordinates 1 vs Many encounters, ensuring one enemy attacks while others dynamically flank and stall[cite: 1].
  • Mechanical Depth: The gameplay emphasizes strict frame data, precise parry windows, orbital spacing, and heavy hit-stop with slow-mo KO pings[cite: 1].

The Combat Deck

Custom Node-Based Pipeline

To allow for deep customization, I built a modular pipeline tool allowing players to mix and match martial arts techniques to create highly personalized, stance-based fighting styles tailored to their preferred rhythm[cite: 1].

  • Stealing Abilities: Players build their custom kit with passive and active abilities by stealing one of their enemies' many abilities after beating them in a fight[cite: 1].
  • Visual Pipeline: The node-based editor translates visual node connections directly into the game's underlying Finite State Machine, ensuring robust scalability.

Tech Art: Sobel Rendering

Comic-Book Edge Detection

The visual identity utilizes simple, low-poly graphics enhanced by a custom Sobel shader to create thick, comic-book-style outlines[cite: 1].

  • Cel-Shaded Aesthetics: Analyzes scene depth and normal data to draw stylized outlines around characters and environmental hazards, creating a vibrant, cel-shaded look[cite: 1].
  • Visual Readability: This approach drastically maximizes visual readability during chaotic, fast-paced combat, pairing perfectly with stylized 2D hit-sparks and dynamic camera snaps[cite: 1].
