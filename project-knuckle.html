Knuckle-House: Combat & Tools

About The Game

Knuckle-House is a 3D roguelike brawler that blends intense arena combat with tactical, run-based progression. Fight your way through a branching map of challenging encounters, taking on groups of intelligent enemies in brutal, martial-arts-style brawls. Every fight tests your reflexes and crowd-control skills as you survive waves of attackers. Manage your persistent health between rounds, spend your rewards to grow stronger, and see how far you can survive the gauntlet.

Play the Prototype on Itch.io

Systems: Frame-Precise Combat & AI

The moment-to-moment combat is built on traditional 3D fighting game fundamentals, emphasizing strict frame data, precise parry windows, and heavy hit-stop. Fights initiate with a strategic Rock-Paper-Scissors phase that dictates the opening momentum. To handle the chaos of group fights, I engineered a central director AI that coordinates 1 vs Many encounters, ensuring one enemy attacks while others dynamically flank and stall.

The "Combat Deck" Pipeline Tool

To allow for rapid iteration and deep customization, I built a modular pipeline tool. Players build and modify their own attack sequences using a customizable "Combat Deck". This node-based system allows players (and designers) to mix and match martial arts techniques to create highly personalized, stance-based fighting styles without touching hardcoded logic.

Tech Art: Myth-Tech Shaders & Visuals

The visual identity utilizes simple, low-poly graphics enhanced by a custom Sobel shader to create thick, comic-book-style outlines. This cel-shaded approach maximizes visual readability during fast-paced combat, pairing perfectly with the stylized 2D hit-sparks, impact frames, and slow-mo KO pings.
