Unreal Engine Environment Design

Project Overview

This project is a high-fidelity environment built in Unreal Engine, showcasing advanced technical art workflows. The primary goal was to establish a strong visual narrative through environmental storytelling while optimizing scene assembly and rendering.

Technical Implementation

Instead of manually placing every prop, I heavily utilized Procedural Content Generation (PCG) tools within Unreal. This allowed for a highly scalable, non-destructive workflow to generate complex environmental details organically.

To bring the scene to life, I focused on dynamic lighting setups, carefully balancing light and shadow to guide the viewer's eye. Finally, I applied advanced post-processing and color grading techniques to unify the visual composition and achieve a polished, cinematic look.
