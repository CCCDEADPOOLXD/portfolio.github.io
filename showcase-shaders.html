Shader Development Showcase

A technical breakdown of custom rendering solutions, Shader Graph implementations, and post-processing logic built to optimize performance and drive core visual mechanics.

Thermal Vision (X-Ray)

Developed specifically for the gunner role in an asymmetric multiplayer setup, providing a crucial tactical advantage through geometry.

  • Full Screen Render Feature: Built using URP's custom render passes to apply post-processing logic globally across the camera view.
  • Layer Masking & Tags: Implements strict layer and tag separation to accurately identify and isolate enemy data from environmental geometry.
  • X-Ray Depth Logic: Bypasses standard depth culling for designated layers, allowing enemy heat signatures to render flawlessly through physical walls and cover.
🎮 View Project: Rotor Warfare

Sobel Outline Shader

A custom edge-detection solution designed to achieve a striking, comic-book aesthetic that improves readability during fast-paced melee combat.

  • Dual-Shader Approach: Combines two distinct shader logics. One relies on a custom HLSL Sobel function, while the other processes depth buffers.
  • Normal-Based Detection: Samples the URP Normal Buffer using screen position data to accurately trace hard geometric edges and creases.
  • Full Screen Post-Process: Calculates outlines dynamically as a full-screen render pass, keeping performance costs low regardless of scene complexity.
🎮 View Project: Knuckle

Impact Frame Shader

Designed to give heavy attacks a devastating sense of weight by syncing rendering overrides with combat animation logic.

  • C# Integration: Triggered dynamically via C# scripts during specific hit-stop (time freeze) animation frames.
  • Material Overrides: Overrides standard materials momentarily to flash the screen with high-contrast, stylized impact colors.
  • Artistic Control: Exposes color clamping properties to the inspector, allowing artists to easily tweak the "punchiness" of physical hits.
🎮 View Project: Knuckle

2D Wind Shader

A highly optimized solution created to breathe life into 2D environments without relying on expensive physical simulations.

  • Vertex Displacement: Manipulates the geometry of 2D sprite assets in real-time.
  • Procedural Noise: Driven by a combination of Time and Noise nodes to simulate organic, rolling wind patterns across foliage and cloth.
  • GPU Performance: Moves all wind calculation entirely to the GPU, ensuring zero overhead on the CPU logic thread.
