VFX & Particles Showcase

Focusing on combat "juice" and game feel, these systems tie together Unity's particle engines, custom shaders, and C# state machines to deliver satisfying and highly optimized visual feedback.

Combat Hit Feedback System

Creating satisfying melee combat requires more than just a particle pop. I engineered a unified hit-reaction system that triggers multiple synchronized events the exact frame a hitbox connects. This includes directional hit sparks, physical mesh vibrations on the enemy, weapon joint trails, camera hit-stop (time freezing), haptic controller feedback, and UI reactions (health bar glowing and bleeding).

🎮 See it in action: Knuckle

Optimized Battlefield Explosions

Because Rotor Warfare requires smooth performance across both PC and mobile Android devices simultaneously, environmental VFX had to be highly optimized. These explosion systems utilize layered particle emitters for smoke and fire, tuned to maintain low overdraw and minimal texture memory footprint while still providing clear tactical feedback on the battlefield.

🎮 See it in action: Rotor Warfare

Dynamic Weapon Trails

To help players read attack arcs and understand hitboxes during fast-paced encounters, I implemented a dynamic trail system. These trails are cast directly from the attacking character's weapon joints, strictly bound to the active frames of the attack animation to ensure the visual effect matches the underlying collision logic perfectly.
