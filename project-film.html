Cinematic Lighting Supervision

Project Overview

For this cinematic short film, the primary goal was to establish a highly atmospheric and visually striking experience. The art direction relied heavily on distinct silhouettes, dramatic shadows, and a carefully controlled color palette to convey the narrative's emotional tone.

My Role & Workflow

Operating in a supervisory capacity, I was responsible for the overall visual "vibe" and lighting of the scenes. My core focus was balancing light values, directing the mood, and ensuring a cohesive, high-fidelity cinematic look was maintained across all shots.
